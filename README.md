I build AI systems that read medical images and clinical data — from grading papilledema in retinal OCT scans to
optimizing clinical laboratories with multi-agent reinforcement learning. Every repository here is a
**research compendium**: paper, code, data (or how to get it), and the reported results in machine-readable form,
so the work can be checked and reused.

## Research areas

| Area | Topics |
|---|---|
| 🩺 **Medical Imaging AI** | Papilledema & retinal disease (OCT, fundus), skin cancer, breast cancer (histopathology, thermography), malaria, endoscopy |
| 📈 **Biosignals** | ECG, EEG seizure detection, photoplethysmography, echocardiography |
| 🧠 **Clinical ML** | Risk prediction, explainable AI (SHAP), multi-criteria model selection, statistical validation |
| 🤖 **Intelligent Systems** | Multi-agent RL, digital twins, predictive maintenance of medical equipment |
| ♿ **Assistive Technology** | Balance & mobility aids, wearable sensing, rehabilitation systems |
